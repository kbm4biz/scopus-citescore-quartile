The displayed Scopus percentile is used first. If it is missing, rank/total produces an estimated percentile and an Estimated CiteScore Quartile. A displayed percentile always wins. On the Scopus page, every result badge also shows the percentile-point distance to the next better quartile; Q1 is marked as highest. A segmented 0–100 scale marks the source's position within the four quartile ranges.

CiteScore-based only—not JCR or SCImago/SJR. Quartiles may differ by subject category.
